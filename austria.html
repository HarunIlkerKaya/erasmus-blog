Austria

Austria was one of the countries I visited during my Erasmus journey, and the city I explored was Salzburg. The city was beautiful, but what impressed me the most was its nature. One of my favorite memories from Salzburg was sitting on a bench and enjoying the view. In front of us were farms, animals, and people taking care of them. Behind them stood a huge mountain, creating an unforgettable landscape. It was such a peaceful place that I could have stayed there for hours just enjoying the scenery. Another highlight of my visit was Salzburg Castle. The castle itself was beautiful, and the view from the top was amazing. Looking over the city and the surrounding mountains was a great experience and one of the most memorable moments of my time in Austria.
